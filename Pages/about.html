Khwezi's Story

"Cooking is something I have always enjoyed doing and the initial interest stemmed from seeing how my late mother would meticulously and lovingly prepare every delicious meal. When I turned 10 years of age my mother finally started teaching me after I had been constantly nagging her on when I will start cooking on my own. I became a chef through her guidance, self-education and a love for good food.
This passion for cooking started to intensify in 2020 during the pandemic where I started exploring making food items from scratch amidst the lockdown. Over time, I honed my skills through practice, experimenting, and learning from various resources. This journey led me to opening my small catering business: Khwezi’s Mobile Kitchen.
Cooking became more than a hobby; it became my profession. While it wasn't initially what I planned, my passion for food and creating culinary experiences drove me to pursue it as a career."

Our Mission

Our mission is to bring organic and fresh food to the community.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become the town’s go to catering company and the leading independent catering company in South Africa in the next six years.
